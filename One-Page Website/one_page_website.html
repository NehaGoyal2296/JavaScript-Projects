Welcome to Canada!

Canada is a beautiful country with many interesting places to visit. It is famous for its mountains, lakes, forests, cities, and waterfalls.

On this website, you can learn about some popular places in Canada and see a few pictures and videos.

Popular Places in Canada

Niagara Falls

Niagara Falls is one of the most famous attractions in Canada. It is located in Ontario and attracts many visitors every year.

Banff National Park

Banff is located in Alberta. It is famous for beautiful mountains, lakes, and amazing natural scenery.

Toronto

Toronto is one of Canada's largest cities. The CN Tower is one of its most famous landmarks.
